The four classes 课程说明

Four classes, four intensities.四节课，四种强度。

  • Morning Flow — mat-based flow and joint wake-up. Fine on an empty stomach, and you can eat breakfast straight after.晨练流动：垫上流动与关节唤醒，空腹也能上。
  • Midday Strength — dumbbells and a bodyweight circuit; opens up the shoulders and back of people who sit all day.午间力量：哑铃与自重循环，适合久坐的人。
  • After-Work Boxing — a ten-minute warm-up, then three combinations. The sweatiest class of the week.下班搏击：十分钟热身 + 三段组合拳。
  • Evening Stretch — wall stretches and breathing; you can go home and sleep when it ends.舒缓拉伸：靠墙拉伸与呼吸练习，助眠。

New to training? Two or three Morning Flow classes first, then move into strength. 新手建议先上「晨练流动」两到三次，再进力量课。
