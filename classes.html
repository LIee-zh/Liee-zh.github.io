Four classes, four intensities

  • Morning Flow — mat flow and joint wake-up. Fine on an empty stomach.
  • Midday Strength — dumbbells and a bodyweight circuit; good for people who sit all day.
  • After-Work Boxing — ten-minute warm-up, then three combinations. The sweatiest hour.
  • Evening Stretch — wall stretches and breathing; you can go home and sleep.

New to training? Two or three Morning Flow classes first.
