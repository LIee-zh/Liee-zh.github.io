A timeline

  • 1997 — Born on 14 October in Tongliang, Chongqing.
  • 2016 — A teacher told her to try the acting exams. She did, and chose the Shanghai Theatre Academy over a first place elsewhere.
  • 2018 — Her first drama aired. She became a working actress.
  • 2020 — Lovely Us and Huang Chengzi gave her a first small following.
  • 2022 — New Life Begins passed 8,500 heat within a day of launch, and Li Wei made her a known name.
  • 2024 — Lin'an in Guardians of the Dafeng; also the voice of Sophie in Howl's Moving Castle.
  • 2026 — Four dramas in one year, from March to October, and a song on the CCTV Spring Festival Gala in February.
