How it works 怎么运作

Three steps, one hour.三步，一小时。

  1. Book online — the places you see are the places that are actually free, so there is no phone call to confirm.
  2. Train for 45 minutes — eight people maximum, so the coach learns your name.
  3. Drink downstairs — show your wristband for ¥8 off a coffee, then sit by the window until you stop sweating.

No membership, no deposit and no day-before confirmation needed. 整个过程不需要年卡、不需要押金，也不需要提前一天打招呼。
